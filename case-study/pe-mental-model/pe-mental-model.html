Key UX insightThis is not a linear pipeline. IC pushback (WF3) reopens the model (WF2), and new data in WF2 can invalidate the revenue build (WF1) — loops happen constantly.
How they think

The question behind each workflow

What deal-team members are really trying to answer — and where it hurts.
Customer Analysis

Is this revenue real and durable?

Approach

Like a prosecutor: assume the revenue is fake until they can prove otherwise.

Key anxiety

Is churn accelerating? Is the top customer leaving? Are margins real per segment?

Decision gate

NRR > 100%? LTV / CAC > 3×? If yes, the revenue thesis holds. If not, reprice or walk.

Biggest pain

Data is never clean. Getting customer data from target CFOs takes days; shaping it into usable cohorts takes more.

Financial Modelling

What price can I pay and still hit my return hurdle?

Approach

Work backwards from a target IRR (typically 20–25%+) and solve for the maximum justifiable entry multiple.

A living document

Every diligence finding — customer churn, margin normalisation — flows back into the model.

Key anxiety

Am I buying at peak margins? Is EBITDA normalised correctly? Will covenants hold in the downside?

Biggest pain

Managing model versions across a team, linking assumptions across tabs, and rebuilding mid-diligence when numbers restate.

IC Documentation

Can I get the IC to say yes?

Approach

Audience-first: what will the IC ask, and which risks will they push back on hardest?

Politics

The thesis slide is the most political — partners debate framing, word choice and narrative angle.

What IC cares about

The deck runs 30–60 pages, but the IC only cares about the thesis, key risks and the return scenario.

Biggest pain

Last-mile assembly — stitching Excel charts into PowerPoint, version chaos, and constant late-stage number updates.
